Ngozi Ihemelandu

As a Data Scientist with Task Impetus Inc., I worked on data-driven decision support projects that focused on applying scientific research to solve real-world problems and improve decision-making. I explored published research for ideas that could offer better ways to address practical challenges, adapted them to specific problems, and used data and experimentation to evaluate how well they worked. My goal was to turn scientific findings into practical, evidence-based solutions that help organizations make better-informed decisions. I am also an independent researcher; my research is in the domain of supply chain and recommender systems and focuses on developing data-driven decision-support frameworks that remain reliable when the available information provides an incomplete representation of reality.
